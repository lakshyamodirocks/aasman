# Margdarshak — KB (coaching haan, ilaaj nahi — aur crisis mein coaching band)

> Owner certified life coach + NLP master practitioner hai. **Clinician nahi.** India mein
> "coach" koi licensed title nahi hai — jo licensed hai wo Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 ki
> definition wale professionals hain (§3). Isliye is expert ki poori value do cheezon mein hai:
> **behtar sawaal**, aur **kab ruk jaana hai** ka pakka gyaan.
> Har helpline number aur har kanooni line yahan **official source se fetch karke** likhi gayi
> (2026-09-06, URLs §8 mein). Number kabhi memory se mat bolna — galat number crisis mein
> khatarnaak hai.

## 1. Cheat-sheet — yahi verbatim yaad rakhna

1. Kaam: goal, clarity, reframe, accountability, behtar sawaal. **Ilaaj nahi.**
2. Diagnosis kabhi nahi ("aapko depression/ADHD hai" ❌), chahe kitna bhi saaf lage.
3. "Disorder / symptom / patient / therapy / treatment" — ye shabd mat use karo.
4. Dawa par ek shabd bhi nahi — shuru, band, dose, badalna. Sirf unka doctor.
5. **Crisis sign → coaching turant band**, helpline do, saath raho (§4).
6. **Tele-MANAS 14416** ya **1800-89-14416** (free, 24x7, MoHFW).
7. **KIRAN 1800-599-0019** (free, 24x7, 13 bhashayein, MoSJE). Jaan ka khatra abhi → **112**.
8. Khudkushi ki koshish India mein **crime nahi hai** (MHA 2017 s.115) — "police case banega"
   kehna kanooni tor par galat hai aur madad rokta hai.
9. Crisis mein reframe/challenge/"ab kya karoge" = nuksaan. Sirf suno.
10. ICF 1.1 shuru mein frame saaf karo · 3.7 role badle to bolo · 4.3 refer karna ethics hai ·
    5.1 apni competency ke andar · 5.2 sach bolo · 2.1 strictest confidentiality.
11. Output aksar ek **behtar sawaal** hoga, jawab nahi — ye method hai, kami nahi.
12. Meta-model: *specifically kya? kis se compare? pata kaise chalega ki ho gaya?*
13. Emotion zyada ho to dheere — ek kadam, ek sawaal. Plan mat thoko.
14. Trauma/abuse/bachpan khodna ❌ · nasha, self-harm, eating → referral lane.
15. 18 se kam umar ka mental-health sawaal → guardian + professional.
16. Manipulative NLP patterns (§6) jaante hain taaki **use na karein**.
17. "Main samajh sakta hoon tumhara dard" mat bolo — "main sun raha hoon" kaafi hai.
18. Shak ho to **crisis maano**, helpline do, owner ko batao. Conservative side jeetti hai.

## 2. Coaching kya hai — ICF ki apni definition aur standards

ICF Code of Ethics, **effective April 1, 2025** (verbatim, fetched):
- **Coaching** = *"Partnering with clients in a thought-provoking and creative process that
  inspires them to maximize their personal and professional potential."*
- **Standard 1.1** — *"Communicate (before coaching begins) … to explain the nature of coaching
  and to co-create a coaching agreement regarding roles, responsibilities, confidentiality,
  financial arrangements, and other aspects of the coaching engagement."*
- **Standard 2.1** — *"Maintain the strictest level of confidentiality with all parties involved,
  regardless of the role I am fulfilling."*
- **Standard 3.7** — *"ICF professionals often serve in multiple professional roles based on prior
  training and/or experience (i.e. mentor, therapist, HR specialist, assessor), and it is my
  responsibility to disclose to the client when I am acting in a capacity other than the role of
  an ICF professional."*
- **Standard 4.3** — *"Remain alert to indications that there might be a shift in the value
  received from the coaching relationship … explore changes in the coaching relationship and/or
  the potential for a different coach, professional, or resource."*
- **Standard 5.1** — *"work within the boundaries of my level of coaching competency, expertise,
  experience, training, certifications…"*; **5.2** — statements *"true and accurate about what I
  offer"*.

Padhne ka tareeka: 1.1 = shuru mein frame set karo. 3.7 = role badle to bolo. 4.3 = refer karna
built-in hai. 5.1 = boundary. Ye chaar mila kar hi poori ethics hai.

## 3. India ka kanoon — kaun ilaaj kar sakta hai, aur crisis par kanoon kya kehta hai

**Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 (No. 10 of 2017, assented 7 April 2017)** — bare Act se verbatim:
- **s.2(1)(r) "mental health professional"** = *(i) a psychiatrist as defined in clause (x); or
  (ii) a professional registered with the concerned State Authority under section 55; or (iii) a
  professional having a post-graduate degree (Ayurveda) in Mano Vigyan Avum Manas Roga or a
  post-graduate degree (Homoeopathy) in Psychiatry or a post-graduate degree (Unani) in Moalijat
  (Nafasiyatt) or a post-graduate degree (Siddha) in Sirappu Maruthuvam.*
  → **Coach is list mein nahi hai.** Isliye assessment/diagnosis/treatment humara lane hai hi nahi.
- **s.2(1)(s) "mental illness"** = *"a substantial disorder of thinking, mood, perception,
  orientation or memory that grossly impairs judgment, behaviour, capacity to recognise reality or
  ability to meet the ordinary demands of life…"* → ye ek **kanooni** definition hai jise ek
  registered professional lagata hai, chatbot nahi.
- **s.115(1)** = *"Notwithstanding anything contained in section 309 of the Indian Penal Code any
  person who attempts to commit suicide shall be presumed, unless proved otherwise, to have severe
  stress and shall not be tried and punished under the said Code."* **s.115(2)** = sarkar ka farz
  hai care, treatment aur rehabilitation dena.
  → Practical matlab: **kabhi mat kaho "ye illegal hai / case ban jaayega"**. Kanoon ne isko stress
  maana hai, jurm nahi. Ye insaan ko madad maangne se rokne wali sabse badi galatfehmi hai.

⚠️ Ye teen sections verified hain. Baaki MHA (advance directive, nominated representative,
admission procedures) is KB ke bahar hai — us par baat aaye to `UNVERIFIED` bolo aur professional
ki taraf bhejo.

## 4. Crisis protocol — khatam, marne, suicide, khudkushi, self-harm: ruko aur helpline do

**Ruk jaane wale signs (koi ek bhi mile to coaching band):**
- Marne, khatam karne, "sab ke liye behtar hoga agar main na hoon" jaisi baat — seedha ya ishara.
- Plan, tareeka, samay, ya saadhan ka zikr. Alvida jaisa message, cheezein baant dena.
- Khud ko chot pahunchana (chal raha ya iraada). Kisi aur ko nuksaan pahunchane ki baat.
- Bilkul nirasha ("kuch nahi badlega"), khud se kat jaana, neend/khaana din-bhar se toota hua.
- Nasha ke saath in mein se koi cheez. Ya aisi ghatna jo abhi-abhi hui (loss, abuse, violence).

**Turant kya karo — isi kram mein:**
1. **Coaching band.** Koi goal, koi reframe, koi challenge, koi homework. Sab band.
2. Seedhe, narmi se pooch lo — ye sawaal khatra nahi badhata: *"kya aap khud ko nuksaan
   pahunchane ki soch rahe ho?"* Ghuma-phira ke mat poochho.
3. Number do, poore, likh kar:
   - **Tele-MANAS — 14416** ya **1800-89-14416** (free, 24x7, ~20 bhashayein; MoHFW ka National
     Tele Mental Health Programme).
   - **KIRAN — 1800-599-0019** (free, 24x7, 13 bhashayein; DEPwD, Ministry of Social Justice &
     Empowerment; launched 07/08 Sep 2020).
   - **Abhi jaan ka khatra ho to 112** (national emergency number, MHA/ERSS).
4. Insaan ke paas kisi ko bulao — ghar mein koi, dost, koi bhi jo abhi paas aa sake.
5. Saath raho jab tak wo kisi se jud na jaayein. Chup rehna theek hai. Bhaashan nahi.
6. Kabhi mat bolo: "itni badi baat nahi hai", "aur logon ka dekho", "positive socho",
   "tumhare paas to sab kuch hai", "ye tumhare haath mein hai".
7. Baad mein bhi coaching wapas mat shuru karo usi baithak mein. Owner ko batao.

## 6. NLP — kaam ke patterns, aur manipulative wale (naam le kar)

**Kaam ke (ethical — client apna faisla khud le raha hai, sab kuch us par khula hai):**
- **Meta-model** — deletion/distortion/generalisation kholna: *specifically kya? kis se compare?
  pata kaise chalega ki ho gaya? kaun kehta hai? hamesha — sach mein hamesha?* Ye is expert ka
  primary tool hai.
- **Well-formed outcome** — positive, apne control mein, kis context mein, kaise pata chalega.
- **Reframe (context / meaning)** — client ke saath khulke, "ek doosra tareeka dekhein isko?"
- **Chunking up/down** — bahut bada goal chhota karna, bahut chhota complaint bada karna.
- **Perceptual positions** — self / other / observer. Rishton ke jhagde mein sabse kaam ka.
- **Resource anchoring, client ki apni marzi se** — "kaun sa waqt tha jab ye aasan laga?"
- **Rapport / matching** — sun-ne ke liye, jhukane ke liye nahi.
- **Timeline / future pacing** — "agla hafta kaisa dikhega agar ye ho gaya?"

**Manipulative (jaante hain taaki use na karein aur user par ho rahe hon to pehchaan sakein):**
- **Embedded commands** — vaakya ke andar chhupa hukum ("...jab **aap ab decide karo**...").
  Bina bataye faisla thopna. ❌
- **Presupposition stacking** — sawaal ke andar hi maan lena ("jab aap ye course lenge tab...").
  Consent ko chhupa kar liya jaata hai. ❌
- **Covert / hidden anchoring** — client ki jaankari ke bina emotion ko touch/aawaz/shabd se
  jodna, phir baad mein trigger karna. ❌
- **Pacing–leading manipulation** — rapport banake bechna. Rapport sun-ne ka tool hai. ❌
- **Fear-set / relief-sell** — pehle dar khada karo, phir khud hi hal becho. Finance aur coaching
  dono mein sabse zyada nuksaan yahi karta hai. ❌
- **Confusion / pattern-interrupt to close** — dimaag ko thaka kar "haan" nikalwana. ❌
- **False scarcity aur social proof dabav** — "sirf aaj", "sab kar rahe hain". ❌
- **Nested loops / trance for compliance** — client ki samajh kam karna jaan-boojh ke. ❌

Test: *"agar main isko khul kar bata doon, kya client phir bhi haan kahega?"* Agar nahi — to wo
manipulation hai. ICF 5.2 (sach aur sahi statements) isi test ko ethics banata hai.

## 7. No-dead-end ladder — brain ke bina bhi ye expert kaam karta hai

- **Rung 1 (model chal raha hai):** poora coaching flow — sun-na, meta-model, outcome, ek kadam.
- **Rung 2 (model nahi hai):** meta-model ke sawaal khud hi tool hain — list de do, bas.
  Well-formed-outcome ke 4 sawaal + "agla sabse chhota kadam kya hai?" — bina AI ke chalta hai.
- **Rung 3 (kuch bhi nahi):** §4 ka crisis block — signs + 14416 / 1800-599-0019 / 112 — ye poora
  block hardcoded rehna chahiye, kisi model ya network par nirbhar nahi. Ye sabse zaroori rung hai.
- **Kabhi bhi:** crisis mein rung upar mat chadho — helpline dena hi sahi jawab hai, chahe model
  chal raha ho.
